**Cord Removal Ritual: Unplugging Energetic Attachments (personal favourite of mine!)**

**Why We Do This:**
Energetic cords are invisible attachments formed between you and other people, places, or situations. These cords can carry energy back and forth, influencing your thoughts, emotions, and energy levels. While some cords are healthy and mutually supportive, others are draining or unbalanced.
Cord removal is about unplugging these connections at the root - not cutting them - to ensure they don't regrow. This process is done with love, intention, and reverence, releasing both parties with gratitude and compassion. It's an act of reclaiming your energy and sovereignty while respecting the connection's role in your life.

**Symbolism of Cord Removal:**
• Roots Unplugged: Represents releasing attachments from their source, allowing true freedom.
• Energy Sovereignty: Symbolizes returning to your own energetic wholeness.
• Compassionate Release: Honours the role of the connection without creating resistance or harm.

**How to Practice Cord Removal:**
1. Find a Quiet Space: Sit in a calm, undisturbed space where you can meditate.
2. Set Your Intention: Say; "I am here to lovingly release any energetic cords that are no longer serving my highest good. I do so with compassion and gratitude for all lessons learned."
3. Visualize the Cords:
• Close your eyes and imagine energetic cords connecting you to the person, place, or situation you wish to release.
• Notice where in your body you feel the cords attached (common areas include the heart, solar plexus, or throat).
4 Unplug the Cords:
• Visualize yourself gently unplugging the cords from your body, like removing a plug from an outlet.
• Imagine the other end of the cord detaching from the other party and dissolving into light.
5. Cleanse and Refill:
• Visualize golden or white light filling the areas where the cords were removed, healing and sealing your energy field.
• Imagine the other person or situation surrounded by light as well, sending love and gratitude for the role they played in your journey.
6. Close the Ritual:
• Take a deep breath and say; "I release you with love. I reclaim my energy, fully and completely."
• End with a cleansing practice (e.g., smudging, using sound bowls, or washing your hands with intention).

Self-reflection: Who or what in your life might you feel ready to lovingly release? How would unplugging these cords create space for your growth and renewal?
